# Photo credits

Every photo shipped with Saksham is listed here and mirrored in `src/lib/photos.ts`, which drives the on-screen
"Sample photo" label, the credit overlay and the alt text (English and Nepali). The public list is at `/credits`.

Rules (from the product brief):
- Only own, consented, or commercially licensed photos.
- Never present stock or stand-in photos as real providers, clients or testimonials.
- Stand-ins are labelled **"Sample photo"** on screen.
- Served as sized AVIF/WebP via next/image, lazy-loaded except the first visible image.

All photos below come from Wikimedia Commons, except `it-professional.webp` from Pexels. They were downscaled (max 1600px) and converted to WebP;
under CC BY-SA those resized files are shared under the same licence. Downloaded 2026-11-17.
